Notes from the wanderer

I try to make my life feel like a show worth following.

Being neurospicy and chronically ill can make the real world difficult to move through, so I create other ways into it.

I investigate haunted houses for the fear and the comedy. I return to Palia for home and community. I write horror, psychological horror, and fantasy to reshape what hurts and ask what a person becomes under pressure. I take pictures to make ordinary moments cinematic, and write lyrics to give feelings rhythm and voice.

This is not the résumé room. It is the ongoing series where all of those interests collide. TessDevillier.com holds the professional proof when someone wants to hire, partner with, or back what comes next.
